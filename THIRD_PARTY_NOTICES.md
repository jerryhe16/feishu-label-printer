# Third-party assets

`assets/fonts/Doto.ttf` is an unmodified font from [Google Fonts / Doto](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/doto), designed by the [Doto project](https://github.com/oliverlalan/Doto). It is distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; see `assets/fonts/OFL.txt` for the complete copyright and license text.

`assets/sample-logo.png` is a generic demonstration image. No customer or company logo is distributed with this project.

Pillow, ReportLab and qrcode are installed as dependencies, not vendored source. pytest and zxing-cpp are optional test dependencies. Their respective licenses apply. Windows system fonts and printer drivers are supplied by the user and are not redistributed here.
